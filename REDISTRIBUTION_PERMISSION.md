# Redistribution Permission

This document records the written permission granted by the intellectual property holders of the original SPRING software (UIUC #2018-073) to redistribute SPRING2 as a modernized non-commercial derivative.

## Summary

In May 2026, the author of SPRING2 (Amir Valizadeh, Yale University) obtained written authorization from the relevant intellectual property holders to publicly distribute SPRING2 for non-commercial research use. Authorization was confirmed by:

- **Imelda Oropeza**, Stanford University Office of Technology Licensing (May 2026)
- **Svetlana Vranic-Sowers**, Office of Technology Management, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (May 2026)

The co-inventors of the original SPRING algorithm — **Shubham Chandak**, **Mikel Hernaez**, and **Idoia Ochoa** — were included in the correspondence and expressed support for the redistribution.

## Scope

This authorization covers the **non-commercial research use** distribution of SPRING2 on public platforms (including GitHub and conda-forge) under the original SPRING Research Use license, with all copyright notices, patent notices (US12300358B2), and attribution requirements preserved.

Commercial use remains subject to a separate license from the intellectual property holders (University of Illinois and Stanford University).
